Search box: Up/Down (hold to repeat) pick a result, Enter opens it, the top result is the default
Search box opens centred and can be dragged (the move cursor shows where); it reopens where you left it
New option PinLayout: pinned recipes start at the right, left or top edge
New option PinArrangement: pins tile under the first one (default) or cascade one title bar apart
New option PinGap: tiled pins leave a gap between them; turn it off to pack them edge to edge
Drag the first pin while the Codex, the mod loader overlay (F2) or the Escape menu is open and the other pins follow; the move cursor shows where it can be grabbed while the Codex or the overlay is open
Where the first pin is dropped is remembered for Tile and Cascade separately and kept between sessions (new options PinTileOffset, PinCascadeOffset)
Arrange pins button on the recipe window forgets the dropped spot for the current arrangement and puts the pins back on the edge
Pinned windows have a Launch planner button, same as the recipe tab (shown while the Codex is open, like Open and Unpin)
Needs a mod loader with plugin interface v71; on an older loader Codex shows "Needs loader" and stays off
The recipe window's redundant Close button is gone (use the X or Esc)
Launch planner opens the StarRupture Planner's add-item link, adding the recipe's output at one machine's output
Launch planner can open starruptureplanner.com or starrupturecalc.com at the item, or the starrupture-planner.com planner page (option Planner)
starruptureplanner.com opens the item's own page for items its calculator cannot target; starrupturecalc.com now covers eight more items (Aerogel, Basic Fuel, Epoxy, Goethite Powder, Organic Compound, Plastic, Reinforced Frame, Reinforced Housing)
CustomUrlTemplate also takes {name} (the item's display name) and {game} (the game's internal item name) next to {item}
PinLayout, Planner and PinArrangement are now dropdowns on the mod loader's config page
The search box and pin positions are no longer config fields; a Saved state row with a Clear button puts them back at once
Pins stay fully on screen at any resolution or font scale: tiles wrap to the side with room, a cascade takes shorter steps near the bottom, and a saved first-pin spot is kept on screen when the screen size changes
Pins follow the first one with no lag while it is dragged, and cascaded pins keep their title bars in pin order
